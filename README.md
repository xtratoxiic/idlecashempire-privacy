# Idle Cash Empire

Idle Cash Empire is a mobile idle tycoon game built with Unity, where players grow a business empire by unlocking shops, hiring managers, and earning money both online and offline.

This project is designed as a lightweight, mobile-first idle experience with a focus on fast progression, offline rewards, and simple management mechanics.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Unlock and upgrade multiple businesses
- Hire managers to automate income
- Earn money while offline
- Scale profits through upgrades and multipliers
- Designed for short, repeatable play sessions

---
